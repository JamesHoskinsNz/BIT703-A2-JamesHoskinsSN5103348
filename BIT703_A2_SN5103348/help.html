Help & Support

Got a question about a product, your order, or planning your next expedition? We are here to help.

Shipping & Delivery

How fast is delivery within New Zealand?
Standard shipping takes 2–4 business days. Rural deliveries may take an additional 1–2 days. Express overnight shipping is available at checkout for urgent gear needs.

Do you ship internationally?
Currently, we only ship within New Zealand to ensure we maintain our fast, localized service.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day "No Worries" return policy. If your gear doesn't fit or isn't exactly what you needed, return it in unused condition with the original tags for a full refund or exchange.

Expert Gear Advice

Can I get help choosing the right equipment?
Absolutely. Our staff are outdoor experts. If you need personalized recommendations for an upcoming trip, drop us an email with your itinerary and we will put together a custom gear list.
